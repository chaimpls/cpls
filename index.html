Probate Applications Application for the Certificate of Appointment of Estate Trustee, formally known as Probate Applications. This certificate provides the estate trustee the authority to manage or distribute estate assets. Any deficiencies in the will need to be addressed prior to submitting this application or the court will not issue the certificate. The documents required for this application include the application form, the original will, a certified death certificate, a draft certificate, probate tax payment, affidavit of service, affidavit of execution and often additional affidavits, consents and a draft order. The documents need to be properly completed or they will be rejected by the court. From simple wills to incomplete or deficient wills and complex probate filings, our Estates Lawyer can assist you with this entire process, including properly completing and submitting the application and obtaining the court issued certificate.
